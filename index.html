I'm Ayush Raj Sinha, an independent researcher working at the frontier of theoretical physics and artificial intelligence — exploring whether the Internet constitutes a fundamental dimension of reality.

I'm also an astrophotographer, capturing the cosmic scale of existence from Bihar, India — alongside work as a content creator, AI filmmaker, and editor crafting cinematic stories with machine intelligence.

Beyond research and the cosmos, I'm a full stack developer — founder of Europa Crest, an AI ecosystem where I design, build, and ship products end to end.

Original Research
The Internet as an Information Structure
The Internet can be viewed as an information structure that mirrors several fundamental properties of physical spacetime. Rather than claiming they are identical, this framework explores deep structural analogies involving connectivity, information flow, causality, emergence, and geometry—drawing inspiration from Wheeler's "It from Bit", Einstein's Relativity, Shannon's Information Theory, and modern concepts in information physics.
